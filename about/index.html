The people behind your progress

Good people. Good practice.

AMIC Judo brings children and adults together through the Olympic sport of judo in Aberdeen. We are connected to the local Muslim community and welcome people from all backgrounds.

Through engaging training, friendship and teamwork, we help every member grow both on and off the mat, whether they train for fitness, personal development or competition.

Your head coaches

Amal & Djabrail Borziev

Amal and Djabrail Borziev bring over 20 years’ combined judo and wrestling background, with regional and national competitive experience and four years’ combined coaching expertise.

How we practise

More than a technique.

  1. 01

    Respect comes first.

    Look after your partner, listen to your coaches and give each other room to learn.

  2. 02

    Progress is personal.

    A first breakfall or a competition goal: your next step matters, wherever you begin.

  3. 03

    We grow together.

    Good practice is built on trust. Encourage each other and share what you learn.
