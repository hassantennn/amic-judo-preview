Membership information and the details to discuss with the club before you join.

Draft for club review. The information below describes the current website and confirmed membership facts. Items marked “To confirm” require the club’s approval.

Try a first session

Your first session is free and there is no joining fee. Contact the club to arrange a suitable class. An online enquiry does not automatically reserve a place.

Membership and family discounts

Bronze is £20 per month for one session a week, Silver is £32 for two, and Gold is £39 for three. The second family member receives 10% off and the third receives 20% off; contact the club to arrange this.

Finding a suitable class

Use the class timetable to check ages and current eligibility. Some groups have temporary restrictions. Coaches can advise on skill level, advanced juniors and suitability before you attend.

Joining and payments

Membership and payments are arranged directly with the club. This website does not take card details or process payments. Please confirm the full terms with the club before you commit to membership.
