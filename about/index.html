AMIC Judo

AMIC Judo is a fun, safe and interactive club where children and adults can discover the Olympic sport of judo, develop discipline, build confidence and improve athleticism.

Through engaging training, friendship and teamwork, we help every member grow both on and off the mat, whether they train for fitness, personal development or competition.
