BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Your questions.
A good place to start.

01Do I need any experience?

You can enquire as a complete beginner or a returning judoka. The club will help you find a suitable class before you attend.

02Which class is right for my child?

Kiddies is for ages 5–8 (boys and girls), Juniors for ages 9–13 (boys only, temporarily), and Teens & Adults for ages 14+ (boys and men only, temporarily). Age groups are subject to skill level. Contact the club about placement, advanced juniors and eligibility for the Sunday Adults session.

03What happens after I join?

Membership starts only after the first payment is confirmed. You will receive your member reference and next steps, and the club will be notified to arrange your introduction.

04What should I wear?

Ask the club about suitable clothing and equipment before your first session. Kit requirements and any additional costs are still to be confirmed.

05How do monthly payments and cancellation work?

Monthly membership is planned. Prices, billing dates, cancellation, refund and missed-payment terms must be approved before real signup opens.
