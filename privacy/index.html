Your privacy matters.

What this website collects, where an enquiry goes and how to contact us about your information.

Draft for club review. The information below describes the current website and confirmed membership facts. Items marked “To confirm” require the club’s approval.

Information in an enquiry

The contact form asks for your name, email address and message. You can also provide a phone number and class interest. Please do not include sensitive personal or medical information in this form.

Where your message goes

Submitting the form sends these details to FormSubmit for delivery to info@amicjudo.com. The form then redirects to our thank-you page. You can also contact us directly by email or phone.

This website does not offer a member account, online payments or analytics tracking. Hosting and form providers may process technical information as part of delivering their services.

Questions about your information

Contact info@amicjudo.com if you want to ask about information you have sent to the club. For a child’s enquiry, a parent or guardian should contact us.
