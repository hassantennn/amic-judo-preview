BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Your questions.
A good place to start.

01What age can I start judo?

We welcome children from the age of 5, as well as teenagers and adults. It is never too early or too late to start learning judo.

02Do I need previous martial arts experience?

Not at all. Our classes welcome complete beginners as well as those with previous judo, wrestling or martial arts experience. Everyone is welcome, regardless of their starting level.

03Is judo safe?

Safety is our priority. All sessions are supervised by qualified coaches, with techniques taught progressively and adapted to each individual’s age and ability.

04What should I wear to my first session?

For your first session, comfortable sportswear such as a T-shirt and tracksuit bottoms is perfectly fine. Once you decide to continue, we recommend buying a judo uniform (judogi).

05Do I need to be fit or athletic to start?

Absolutely not. Judo is for everyone, regardless of fitness level or athletic ability. Training gradually develops strength, flexibility, coordination, balance and overall fitness.

06Can I join if I only want to improve my fitness?

Of course. Not everyone joins judo to compete. Whether your goal is fitness, confidence, discipline, self-development or simply trying something new, judo has something to offer.

07Can I take part in competitions?

Yes. For those interested in competing, we provide opportunities to develop their skills and take part in local, regional and national competitions. Competition is encouraged but never compulsory.

08Do you offer classes for complete beginners?

Yes. Our sessions cater to different ages and abilities, so beginners can learn the fundamentals while more experienced members keep developing their techniques.

09Can judo help with bullying and self-confidence?

Absolutely. Judo helps develop confidence, resilience and the skills to protect yourself if necessary. Just as importantly, we teach respect, kindness and self-control — standing up for yourself while caring for and supporting others. At AMIC Judo we foster a friendly, welcoming environment where everyone feels safe, included and encouraged to grow.

010How do I join AMIC Judo?

Simply get in touch with our coaching team to enquire about joining. We will be happy to answer your questions and help you find the right class.
